# Domain assumptions

All rows are **PROPOSED**. Task `S0-ASSUMPTIONS` (P1, due 2026-11-29) turns them into `ACCEPTED`, or changes them through a PR. Changing an accepted assumption needs the `contract-change` process (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`).

| ID | Topic | Decision | Rationale | Requirements | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-01 | Roles | GUEST (not logged in), USER, ADMIN (admin = moderator). The "author" is the USER who created a mission. Team roles are TEAM_ADMIN and MEMBER. | Minimal role set that covers every requirement and keeps authorization rules checkable. | R01, R07, R10 | PROPOSED |
| A-02 | Authentication | Email + password (bcrypt). The JWT access token expires after 2 h and is stored in `localStorage`. Each `User` has a `tokenVersion` that goes into the JWT; logout, suspension and password change increment it, which invalidates all outstanding tokens (a real server-side logout). Login and register are rate-limited. | Brief requires protected credentials and server-side authN. A token version gives real logout without a session table. | R01 | PROPOSED |
| A-03 | Personal data | The public profile shows nickname, avatar URL, bio, city and total points. Email and full name are visible only to the user and to admins. | Resolves the brief's "visibility of personal data" ambiguity with data minimisation. | R01 | PROPOSED |
| A-04 | Admin functions | List and search users; suspend or reactivate a user (a suspended user cannot log in, and their PUBLISHED missions become CLOSED); promote or demote admins (never yourself); close or archive any mission. One admin account is seeded. | Concrete meaning of "platform managers need admin functions". | R01 | PROPOSED |
| A-05 | Mission lifecycle | DRAFT → PUBLISHED → CLOSED (no new acceptances; running participations continue) → ARCHIVED (hidden from explore; still visible to the author and to past participants in their history). Only DRAFT missions can be deleted. Archiving is allowed only when no participation is ACTIVE or SUBMITTED. | Clear states make publication, editing and history rules testable. | R02 | PROPOSED |
| A-06 | Editing | DRAFT missions, and PUBLISHED missions never accepted, are fully editable. After the first acceptance only title, teaser, description and conditions can be edited. Objectives, mode, time limit, participation type, team sizes and maxAttempts are locked; capacity can only go up; visibility can only widen (RESTRICTED → PUBLIC). Optimistic locking (a `version` column) prevents races between edit and accept. | Resolves "editing with active participants" without invalidating running attempts. | R02, R04 | PROPOSED |
| A-07 | Secret-timed preview | Before acceptance the user sees: title, teaser, category, time limit, number of objectives, total points, an approximate location (if any), participation type and conditions. Objective titles, descriptions and answers are **never** serialized before acceptance (dedicated DTO mappers, covered by tests). For ORDINARY missions the objectives are visible to anyone who can view the mission. | Lets users decide without leaking the secret; the leak is the most checked security property. | R03, R04 | PROPOSED |
| A-08 | Timer | On acceptance the server stores `deadlineAt = acceptedAt + timeLimit`, using the server `Clock`. Responses carry `deadlineAt` and `serverNow`; the client computes its offset and shows the countdown, but every decision is made on the server. Expiry is evaluated lazily on every read and write, and a background sweeper (every 30 s) marks participations EXPIRED and emits an event. | Authoritative server time defeats local-clock tampering and device changes. | R04 | PROPOSED |
| A-09 | Last-second submissions | A submission counts if the server receives it at most `deadlineAt + 10 s` (a documented grace period for network latency). Anything later gets a 409 with code `DEADLINE_PASSED`. Reviews may happen after the deadline. | Resolves "proofs sent just before the deadline" fairly and deterministically. | R04, R05 | PROPOSED |
| A-10 | Verification types | SELF: auto-approved. ANSWER: normalized (case and whitespace insensitive) and compared with a stored hash; immediate verdict; at most 5 wrong attempts per objective. TEXT_PROOF and IMAGE_PROOF: the author approves, or rejects with a reason; a rejected objective can be resubmitted while time remains. | Coherent verification criteria; answers are never stored in clear. | R05 | PROPOSED |
| A-11 | Outcomes | ACTIVE → SUBMITTED (everything submitted, waiting for reviews) → COMPLETED (all objectives approved) or FAILED (rejections after the deadline, or answer attempts exhausted). ACTIVE → ABANDONED (explicit withdrawal). ACTIVE → EXPIRED (deadline passed with objectives still unsubmitted). ORDINARY missions without a time limit never expire. | Complete state machine for success, abandoned and expired outcomes. | R04, R05 | PROPOSED |
| A-12 | Scoring | Each objective has points (0–100, default 10). Mission points are their sum, multiplied by 1.5 (rounded) for SECRET_TIMED. Points are awarded exactly once, on COMPLETED, through a `PointsLedger` entry. In team missions, every member who contributed at least one approved objective receives the full mission points. | Simple, auditable scoring; the ledger prevents double awards. | R05, R11 | PROPOSED |
| A-13 | Repetition | `maxAttempts` (default 1, max 3). After ABANDONED, EXPIRED or FAILED the user may accept again if attempts remain and the mission is still PUBLISHED; never after COMPLETED. Authors cannot accept their own missions. Archived missions cannot be reopened; the author can duplicate them as a new DRAFT. | Resolves "reopening or repeating a mission". | R04 | PROPOSED |
| A-14 | Withdrawal | An explicit, confirmed, irreversible action. It counts as an attempt and frees the capacity slot. | Resolves "the meaning of withdrawal". | R05, R09 | PROPOSED |
| A-15 | Notifications | Persisted, pushed live through the Socket.IO event `notification:new`, shown with an unread badge, paginated, and can be marked read one by one or all at once. Producers: mission accepted (to the author); participation status changed (to the participant or the team members); proof reviewed; invitation received, responded or revoked; waitlist offer and offer expiry; team invitation; member joined or left; optionally "5 minutes left". | Covers the events the brief lists plus those needed by R08–R12. | R06 | PROPOSED |
| A-16 | Restricted visibility | A RESTRICTED mission is visible only to its author, admins and users in the `MissionAccess` allow-list (granted manually by the author, or by an accepted invitation). Anyone else gets **404** (no existence leak) from detail, objectives, proofs and every related endpoint, and the mission never appears in the list, the map or search results for them. | 404 instead of 403 avoids leaking the existence of restricted content. | R07 | PROPOSED |
| A-17 | Invitations | Only for RESTRICTED missions, sent by nickname. One record per (mission, invitee): re-inviting after DECLINED or REVOKED reopens it as PENDING, while a duplicate PENDING gets 409. Accepting grants access (it does not accept the mission and does not reserve a slot). Revoking a PENDING or ACCEPTED invitation removes the access; a participation already started continues. The author sees the status of every invitation. | Defines revocation and multiple-invitation behaviour required by R08. | R08 | PROPOSED |
| A-18 | Capacity and waitlist | `maxParticipants = null` means unlimited. Occupied slots are participations in ACTIVE, SUBMITTED or COMPLETED; ABANDONED, EXPIRED and FAILED free their slot. For TEAM missions the slots count teams. Last-slot race: a transaction with `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on the mission row, then the count; the loser gets 409 `MISSION_FULL` and is offered the waitlist. The waitlist is FIFO. When a slot frees up, the first WAITING entry becomes OFFERED with a 15-minute hold, and the user must *claim* it, which is the actual acceptance (so a secret timer starts only then). When the hold expires, the offer goes to the next entry. Users can leave the waitlist at any time, and cannot be on the waitlist and active on the same mission. | Row lock serializes the last-slot race; offer-and-claim keeps the secret timer fair. | R09 | PROPOSED |
| A-19 | Teams | Unique name (3–30 chars), at most 6 members. The creator is TEAM_ADMIN. The admin invites members by nickname, and invitees accept or decline. Members can leave; the admin can remove members. The admin must transfer the role before leaving, and if they are the last member the team is disbanded. A user can belong to several teams. | Essential join/leave operations with unambiguous admin succession. | R10 | PROPOSED |
| A-20 | Team participation | A mission has `participationType: INDIVIDUAL \| TEAM`, plus `minTeamSize` and `minContributors`. Only the TEAM_ADMIN accepts on behalf of the team, and the team needs at least `minTeamSize` members. One ACTIVE participation per (mission, team). A user cannot take part in the same mission both individually and through a team, or through two teams (409 `ALREADY_PARTICIPATING`). Progress is shared. Concurrent submissions on the same objective are serialized with optimistic locking (`version`): the loser gets 409 together with the latest state. Completion requires every objective approved **and** at least `minContributors` distinct contributors. Members who leave lose access; their contributions stay. | Keeps shared progress consistent under concurrency. | R11 | PROPOSED |
| A-21 | Live team updates | One Socket.IO room per team participation. Every state change appends a `TeamProgressEvent` with a per-participation, strictly increasing `seq`, in the same DB transaction, and the server emits only after the commit. The client applies only `seq = lastSeq + 1`; on a gap or a reconnection it fetches the snapshot (`GET /api/v1/team-participations/:id` returns the state plus `lastSeq`) and resumes. Online presence is shown. | Gives total ordering and reconnection recovery. | R12 | PROPOSED |
| A-22 | Location | Optional latitude/longitude plus a place name, chosen by clicking on the Leaflet map. There is no geocoding service and no GPS check; browser geolocation is not used. | Demos must not require travel or external services. | R02, R03 | PROPOSED |
| A-23 | Categories | A fixed seeded list: Exploration, Puzzle, Culture, Photography, Creative challenge, Outdoor. | Keeps filters and seeds simple. | R02, R03 | PROPOSED |
| A-24 | Search | Keyword (ILIKE on title and teaser), category, availability (AVAILABLE / FULL / CLOSED), mode, participation type, and the map bounding box. Availability comes from a port that returns AVAILABLE until R09 binds the real implementation. | Meets the minimum filters; the port decouples R03 from R09. | R03, R09 | PROPOSED |
| A-25 | Images | JPEG, PNG or WebP, at most 5 MB. Stored on server disk (`backend/uploads/`, gitignored) under random names, and served only to the participant(s), the author and admins, through an authenticated endpoint. | Proof images are private data; random names avoid guessing. | R05 | PROPOSED |
| A-26 | Validation | The shared hand-written `Validator` on every endpoint (body, params, query), with length limits on every text field. Times are stored in UTC and shown in local time. | One validation mechanism, no extra dependency to justify. | all | PROPOSED |
